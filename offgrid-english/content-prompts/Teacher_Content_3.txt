═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ FALSE COGNATE #1: ACTUALLY / ACTUELLEMENT ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
English Word: actually French Word: actuellement
French Meaning: "Actuellement" in French means "currently" or "at present" (right now).
	•	Example (French): "J'habite actuellement à Yaoundé." = "I currently live in Yaoundé."
English Meaning: "Actually" in English means "in fact" or "really" (used to correct, emphasize, or give new information).
	•	Example (English): "I thought he was from Douala, but actually he's from Bamenda."
Common Mistake:
	•	❌ French speaker says: "I am actually working at the ministry." (Meaning "I am currently working...")
	•	✅ Should say: "I am currently working at the ministry."
	•	✅ Correct use of "actually": "Actually, I prefer tea, not coffee."
Memory Trick:
	•	Actually = En fait (In fact)
	•	Currently = Actuellement (At present)
