# Content Creation Prompt #2: Real-World Cameroonian Scenarios

**Project**: OffGrid English - Offline-First English Learning for Cameroon
**Created for**: ESL Educators / Content Creators
**Date**: October 28, 2025

---

## Purpose

Create **authentic, contextual grammar practice** using real-life situations that Cameroonian learners encounter daily.

Instead of abstract grammar drills, students will practice English through realistic conversations at markets, moto-taxi rides, health clinics, and other familiar contexts. This makes learning immediately useful and culturally relevant.

---

## What Each Scenario Should Include

1. **Scenario Name** (e.g., "At Mokolo Market")
2. **Context Description** (2-3 sentences setting the scene)
3. **Characters** (who's talking - give them Cameroonian names)
4. **Icon/Emoji** (suggest one: 🏪, 🏍️, 🏥, 💰, 🍲, 🖨️, ⛪, 🏠)
5. **6-8 Grammar Questions** targeting different error types

### What Each Question Should Include

- **Dialogue with blank** (fill-in-the-blank format)
- **3 answer options** (one correct, two plausible distractors)
- **Correct answer**
- **Feedback** that references the scenario context
- **Grammar category** (from the 7 modules: Tense, S-V Agreement, Prepositions, Word Order, Plurality, Articles, Auxiliaries)

## Template

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO #1: AT MOKOLO MARKET
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Context:
You're at Mokolo Market in Yaoundé, one of the largest markets in Central
Africa. You want to buy tomatoes, onions, and peppers for making stew.
The vegetable seller (Mama Grace) is arranging her display.

Characters:
- You (the customer)
- Mama Grace (vegetable seller, experienced, friendly)

Icon: 🏪

---

QUESTION 1

Dialogue:
You: "Good morning, Mama! How much ___ these tomatoes?"
Mama Grace: "500 francs per kilo, my dear."

Options:
A) is
B) are
C) be

Correct Answer: B) are

Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement

Feedback:
"Correct! We use 'are' with plural nouns like 'tomatoes.' In markets,
you'll often ask 'How much are these?' or 'How much is this?' Singular
items use 'is' (How much is this pepper?), but multiple items use 'are.'"

Why French speakers struggle (for teacher reference):
In French: "Combien coûtent ces tomates?" uses "coûtent" (plural), but
the English structure with "are" is different.

---

QUESTION 2

Dialogue:
Mama Grace: "These tomatoes ___ fresh from the farm this morning."
You: "They look very good!"

Options:
A) come
B) comes
C) came

Correct Answer: C) came

Grammar Category: Tense & Form

Feedback:
"Correct! We use 'came' (past tense) because the tomatoes arrived 'this
morning' (already happened). In markets, sellers often say 'It came fresh
today' or 'I bought it this morning.' Past actions = past tense!"

Why French speakers struggle:
French passé composé: "Ces tomates sont venues" might make learners think
"are came" in English.

---

QUESTION 3

Dialogue:
You: "Can I pay ___ mobile money?"
Mama Grace: "Yes, I have Orange Money."

Options:
A) by
B) with
C) on

Correct Answer: B) with

Grammar Category: Prepositions

Feedback:
"Correct! We say 'pay WITH mobile money' or 'pay BY mobile money' (both
are acceptable). In Cameroon, it's common to ask 'Can I pay with Orange
Money?' or 'Do you accept MTN MoMo?'"

Why French speakers struggle:
French "payer avec" (with) or "payer par" (by) - both exist, so English
"with" is correct but learners may be uncertain.

---

[Continue with 5 more questions for this scenario...]
```

## Detailed Example: Complete Scenario

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO #2: TAKING A MOTO-TAXI
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Context:
You need to get from Carrefour Nlongkak to the University of Yaoundé I.
It's 4 PM, and traffic is heavy. You see a moto-taxi driver (Jean-Pierre)
waiting at the roadside.

Characters:
- You (passenger)
- Jean-Pierre (moto driver, mid-30s, knows the city well)

Icon: 🏍️

---

QUESTION 1

Dialogue:
You: "Good afternoon. ___ you go to University of Yaoundé I?"
Jean-Pierre: "Yes, I can take you there."

Options:
A) Do
B) Does
C) Are

Correct Answer: A) Do

Grammar Category: Auxiliaries

Feedback:
"Correct! We use 'Do you...' for questions with action verbs like 'go.'
When asking moto drivers, you'll say 'Do you go to...?' or 'Can you take
me to...?' Both are polite and common in Yaoundé."

---

QUESTION 2

Dialogue:
Jean-Pierre: "The fare ___ 500 francs because of the traffic."
You: "That's okay."

Options:
A) is
B) are
C) be

Correct Answer: A) is

Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement

Feedback:
"Correct! 'The fare' is singular, so we use 'is.' Other examples:
'The price is...,' 'The cost is...,' 'The trip is...'"

---

QUESTION 3

Dialogue:
You: "I need to get there ___ 5 PM. Can we make it?"
Jean-Pierre: "Yes, if we leave now."

Options:
A) before
B) until
C) at

Correct Answer: A) before

Grammar Category: Prepositions

Feedback:
"Correct! 'Before 5 PM' means 'earlier than 5 PM.' Use 'before' when you
have a deadline. 'By 5 PM' also works. 'Until' means 'up to that time'
(I'll wait until 5 PM). 'At' is for exact times (I arrive at 5 PM)."

---

QUESTION 4

Dialogue:
Jean-Pierre: "Which route do you prefer? The main road or the shortcut?"
You: "Let's take the shortcut. I ___ in a hurry."

Options:
A) am
B) is
C) are

Correct Answer: A) am

Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement

Feedback:
"Correct! With 'I,' we always use 'am.' Common phrases: 'I am late,'
'I am in a hurry,' 'I am ready.' This is the verb 'to be' (I am, you are,
he/she is)."

---

QUESTION 5

Dialogue:
You: "Thank you. You drive very ___."
Jean-Pierre: "No problem, I know this city well."

Options:
A) careful
B) carefully
C) care

Correct Answer: B) carefully

Grammar Category: Word Order (Adverbs)

Feedback:
"Correct! We use the adverb 'carefully' to describe HOW someone drives.
Adverbs usually end in -ly: drive carefully, speak clearly, walk slowly.
'Careful' is an adjective (a careful driver), but 'carefully' describes
the action."

---

QUESTION 6

Dialogue:
Jean-Pierre: "We ___ at the university. That will be 500 francs, please."
You: "Here you are. Thank you!"

Options:
A) arrive
B) arrives
C) arrived

Correct Answer: C) arrived

Grammar Category: Tense & Form

Feedback:
"Correct! 'Arrived' is past tense because you've just reached the destination.
The trip is finished (past). When you reach somewhere, you can say 'We
arrived' or 'We've arrived' (present perfect)."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
END OF SCENARIO #2
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## Scenario Creation Checklist

For each scenario, ensure:

✅ **Authentic Setting** - Uses real Cameroonian locations, names, contexts
✅ **Natural Dialogue** - How people actually speak in that situation
✅ **Cultural Details** - Specific to Cameroon (prices in francs, local place names)
✅ **Variety of Grammar** - Mix of all 7 categories across the scenario
✅ **Difficulty Range** - 2-3 easy, 3-4 medium, 1-2 challenging per scenario
✅ **Practical Vocabulary** - Words students will actually use in that context
✅ **Positive Tone** - Respectful, realistic, avoids stereotypes
✅ **Clear Feedback** - Explains the grammar rule AND references the scenario

---

## Grammar Distribution Guide

Aim to include all 7 grammar categories across your scenarios:

| Grammar Category | Example Question Context |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| **Tense & Form** | Past actions (Yesterday I went...), Future plans (I will...) |
| **Subject-Verb Agreement** | He/she does, They do, It is, They are |
| **Prepositions** | Location (at the market), Time (in the morning), Method (by taxi) |
| **Word Order** | Adverb placement (I always...), Question order (Do you...?) |
| **Plurality** | Many students, These items, singular vs plural |
| **Articles** | The market, a book, no article (life is beautiful) |
| **Auxiliaries** | Do/does/did, will, can, should |

Try to include 1-2 questions from each category per scenario.

## Tips for Authentic Cameroonian Contexts

### Use Real Locations
- ✅ "Mokolo Market," "Carrefour Nlongkak," "University of Yaoundé I"
- ❌ Generic "the market," "the university"

### Use Realistic Names
- ✅ "Mama Grace," "Jean-Pierre," "Dr. Njoku," "Aminata"
- ❌ Generic "the seller," "the driver"

### Use Local Details
- ✅ "500 francs per kilo," "Orange Money," "MTN MoMo"
- ❌ Generic currency, payment methods

### Reference Real Situations
- ✅ Traffic at 4 PM, rainy season flooding, power cuts
- ❌ Generic situations that could be anywhere

### Include Cultural Norms
- ✅ Polite greetings ("Good morning, Mama"), bargaining
- ❌ Direct, transactional-only dialogue
