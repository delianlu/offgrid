
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SCENARIO #1: AT MOKOLO MARKET ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Context: You are at the busy Mokolo Market in Yaoundé. You need to buy ingredients (tomatoes, onions, and rice) for the evening meal. You find a seller, Mama Ngono, who is arranging her vegetables.
Characters:
	•	You (the customer)
	•	Mama Ngono (vegetable seller, friendly)
Icon: 🏪

QUESTION 1
Dialogue: You: "Good morning, Mama! How much ___ these tomatoes?" Mama Ngono: "They are 500 francs for that pile, my dear."
Options: A) is B) are C) be
Correct Answer: B) are
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "Correct! We must use 'are' because the noun 'tomatoes' is plural. In the market, you ask 'How much are these?' for many items, but 'How much is this?' for a single item (like 'How much is this one onion?')."

QUESTION 2
Dialogue: You: "Okay, I ___ that pile of tomatoes." Mama Ngono: "Good. And the onions?"
Options: A) will take B) am taking C) take
Correct Answer: A) will take
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Future)
Feedback: "That's it! We use 'will take' (or 'I'll take') when you make an instant decision at the moment of speaking. This is very common when shopping."

QUESTION 3
Dialogue: Mama Ngono: "I ___ fresh rice from Garoua yesterday." You: "Oh, good. How much is one gobelet (cup)?"
Options: A) buy B) has bought C) bought
Correct Answer: C) bought
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Past Simple)
Feedback: "Perfect! The word 'yesterday' is a finished past time, so we must use the Past Simple tense, 'bought'. We can't say 'has bought yesterday'."

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: You: "I need two gobelets of rice and some ___." Mama Ngono: "Here you are. The rice is 600 francs."
Options: A) onion B) onions C) of onion
Correct Answer: B) onions
Grammar Category: Plurality (Regular)
Feedback: "Correct! Because you are talking about more than one onion, you must use the plural form 'onions'. In English, we must pronounce the 's' sound, unlike in French where 'oignon' and 'oignons' can sound the same."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: Mama Ngono: "So, that is 500 for tomatoes, 600 for rice... 1100 francs." You: "___ you have change for 2000 francs?"
Options: A) Do B) Are C) Does
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)
Correct Answer: A) Do
Feedback: "Excellent! To ask a 'yes/no' question with the main verb 'have', we must use the auxiliary verb 'Do'. Asking 'You have...?' is a common error from French ('Vous avez...?')."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: You: "This is ___ very nice market. I am new in this quartier." Mama Ngono: "Welcome! Yes, Mokolo is the best."
Options: A) a B) an C) the
Correct Answer: A) a
Grammar Category: Articles
Feedback: "That's right. We use 'a' to introduce a singular, non-specific noun. You are saying it is 'one' nice market. We don't use 'the' unless we are comparing it to others ('the nicest market')."

QUESTION 7
Dialogue: Mama Ngono: "I ___ have many customers on Mondays." You: "Oh, really? I thought Monday was a busy day."
Options: A) not B) don't C) doesn't
Correct Answer: B) don't
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Negatives)
Feedback: "Perfect! To make a negative sentence with 'I', 'you', 'we', or 'they', we must use the auxiliary 'don't' (do not). We cannot say 'I not have...' (which is an error from French 'Je n'ai pas...')."

QUESTION 8
Dialogue: You: "Thank you for the ___." Mama Ngono: "You are welcome! Have a good day!"
Options: A) good service B) service good C) serving good
Correct Answer: A) good service
Grammar Category: Word Order (Adjectives)
Feedback: "Correct! In English, the adjective (describing word) 'good' must come before the noun 'service'. This is the opposite of French, where you might say 'un service bon'."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SCENARIO #2: TAKING A MOTO-TAXI ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Context: You need to get from Carrefour Nlongkak to the Ministry of Education (MINEDUB) building in Yaoundé. It's 4 PM, and traffic is heavy. You see a moto-taxi driver (whom we'll call Jean-Pierre) waiting at the roadside.
Characters:
	•	You (passenger)
	•	Jean-Pierre (moto driver)
Icon: 🏍️

QUESTION 1
Dialogue: You: "Good afternoon. How much to go to MINEDUB?" Jean-Pierre: "Because of the traffic, the fare ___ 700 francs."
Options: A) is B) are C) be
Correct Answer: A) is
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "Correct! We use 'is' because the subject 'the fare' (or 'the price') is singular. Even if the price is 700 francs (plural), the fare itself is one thing, so 'the fare is'."

QUESTION 2
Dialogue: You: "That's too expensive. I ___ 500 francs." Jean-Pierre: "Okay, 500 is fine. Let's go."
Options: A) am having B) have C) has
Correct Answer: B) have
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "That's right. The subject 'I' always pairs with the verb 'have'. We also don't usually say 'I am having' for possession; we use the simple form 'I have'."

QUESTION 3
Dialogue: You: "Can we go ___ the shortcut behind the quartier?" Jean-Pierre: "Yes, that way is faster."
Options: A) on B) through C) at
Correct Answer: B) through
Grammar Category: Prepositions
Feedback: "Good choice! We use 'through' to describe moving from one side to the other inside an area, like a quartier or a tunnel. We go 'on' a main road, but 'through' a shortcut."

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: You: "Please, can you drive ___? I am not in a hurry." Jean-Pierre: "No problem, Mama/Papa. Safety first."
Options: A) slow B) slowly C) more slow
Correct Answer: B) slowly
Grammar Category: Word Order (Adverbs)
Feedback: "Perfect! We need an adverb, 'slowly', to describe the verb 'drive' (HOW should he drive?). 'Slow' is an adjective (a slow driver), but we 'drive slowly'."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: Jean-Pierre: "We ___ at the ministry." You: "That was fast. Thank you."
Options: A) have arrived B) are arrive C) did arrive
Correct Answer: A) have arrived
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Present Perfect)
Feedback: "Correct! We use 'have arrived' (Present Perfect) to announce a recent action that is finished now. It's like saying, 'We are now at the destination'."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: You: "Here is your money. I ___ a helmet." Jean-Pierre: "Ah, sorry, I forgot! I will bring it next time."
Options: A) not wear B) didn't wear C) don't wear
Correct Answer: B) didn't wear
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Negatives)
Feedback: "That's right. The trip is finished, so you must use the past tense. The correct negative in the past is 'didn't wear'. 'Don't wear' is present (I don't wear helmets in general)."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SCENARIO #3: HEALTH CLINIC VISIT ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Context: You are not feeling well and have gone to a local health clinic (Centre de Santé). You are now speaking to the doctor, Dr. Bertrand, to describe your symptoms.
Characters:
	•	You (the patient)
	•	Dr. Bertrand (the doctor)
Icon: 🏥

QUESTION 1
Dialogue: You: "Good morning, Doctor. I ___ sick for three days now." Dr. Bertrand: "I see. Please sit down."
Options: A) am B) have been C) was
Correct Answer: B) have been
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Present Perfect)
Feedback: "Correct! When you describe a situation that started in the past and continues to the present (you are still sick), you must use the Present Perfect ('have been') with 'for' or 'since'. Saying 'I am sick for three days' is a direct translation of the French 'Je suis malade depuis trois jours', which is a very common error."

QUESTION 2
Dialogue: Dr. Bertrand: "___ you have a cough or a fever?" You: "Yes, I have a fever and my head hurts."
Options: A) Do B) Are C) Have
Correct Answer: A) Do
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)
Feedback: "That's right! To ask a 'yes/no' question using the main verb 'have', we must use the auxiliary 'helper' verb 'Do'. Asking 'Are you...' or 'Have you...' (which is less common) are frequent mistakes."

QUESTION 3
Dialogue: Dr. Bertrand: "Okay. And your stomach? ___ it also hurt?" You: "No, my stomach is fine."
Options: A) Do B) Is C) Does
Correct Answer: C) Does
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)
Feedback: "Excellent. The subject is 'your stomach' (it). To ask a 'yes/no' question with 'he', 'she', or 'it', we must use the auxiliary verb 'Does'."

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: You: "What is wrong with me, Doctor?" Dr. Bertrand: "It seems you ___ malaria."
Options: A) are having B) have C) has
Correct Answer: B) have
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "Correct. The subject is 'You', which always pairs with the verb 'have'. We use the simple form 'you have' to talk about possessing an illness, not 'you are having'."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: Dr. Bertrand: "You ___ take this medicine for five days." You: "Okay, I will."
Options: A) must B) must to C) are to
Correct Answer: A) must
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Modals)
Feedback: "Correct! 'Must' is a modal verb used to give a strong order or vital instruction. Remember, after a modal (must, can, should), we use the verb directly ('take'). The error 'must to take' comes from translating the French infinitive 'devoir prendre'."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: You: "Can you give me some ___ for the fever?" Dr. Bertrand: "Yes, this medicine will help with that."
Options: A) advices B) advice C) an advice
Correct Answer: B) advice
Grammar Category: Plurality (Uncountable)
Feedback: "Perfect! 'Advice' is an uncountable noun in English. It never has an '-s' and you cannot say 'an advice'. This is different from the French 'des conseils' (plural)."

QUESTION 7
Dialogue: Dr. Bertrand: "You should also rest ___ home and not go to work." You: "I understand. Thank you, Doctor."
Options: A) in B) on C) at
Correct Answer: C) at
Grammar Category: Prepositions
Feedback: "That's right. We use the preposition 'at' for the fixed expression 'stay at home' or 'rest at home'. We live 'in' a house, but we stay 'at' home."

QUESTION 8
Dialogue: Dr. Bertrand: "The tests ___ that you have malaria." You: "I thought so."
Options: A) shows B) show C) is showing
Correct Answer: B) show
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "Good! The subject is 'The tests' (plural, 'they'). A plural subject takes the base verb 'show'. We only add '-s' ('shows') for a singular subject (e.g., 'The test shows...')."

Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)

QUESTION 2

Correct Answer: B) finished

QUESTION 3

Options: A) a B) an C) Ø (no article)
Correct Answer: B) an
Grammar Category: Articles

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: Mr. Foncha: "Please fill out this registration form. You must return it ___ Friday at the latest." Ngono: "I will bring it tomorrow."
Options: A) in B) at C) on

Options: A) money B) monies C) moneys
Correct Answer: A) money
Grammar Category: Plurality (Uncountable)
Feedback: "That's it. 'Money' is an uncountable noun in English. It never has an '-s' and is always singular (like 'water' or 'rice')."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: Mr. Foncha: "The school fee for the first term ___ 50,000 francs." Ngono: "That is acceptable."
Options: A) are B) is C) be
Correct Answer: B) is
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "Correct! The subject 'The school fee' is singular (one fee), so it must use the singular verb 'is'. Don't be confused by '50,000 francs' (plural); the subject is 'the fee'."

QUESTION 7
Dialogue: Ngono: "Where ___ the office for the bursar (school accountant)?" Mr. Foncha: "It is the second door on the left."
Options: A) is B) does C) are
Correct Answer: A) is
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement / Auxiliaries
Feedback: "That's right. We are asking about the location of the office (a state of being), so we use the verb 'to be'. The subject 'the office' (it) pairs with 'is'."

QUESTION 8
Dialogue: Mr. Foncha: "Our students ___ a uniform, but they must wear it correctly." Ngono: "I understand, Headmaster. Thank you."
Options: A) have B) has C) are having
Correct Answer: A) have
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "Good! The subject is 'Our students' (plural, 'they'). A plural subject always pairs with the verb 'have'. We only use 'has' for he, she, or it."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SCENARIO #5: JOB INTERVIEW ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Context: Tchamba has been invited for a job interview for an administrative assistant position at a small business in Douala. He is now speaking with the manager, Mrs. Ekane.
Characters:
	•	Mrs. Ekane (The Manager)
	•	Tchamba (The Applicant)
Icon: 👔

QUESTION 1
Dialogue: Mrs. Ekane: "Good morning, Tchamba. Please, have a seat. Your CV says you ___ at the University of Buea." Tchamba: "Yes, that's correct. I graduated in 2023."
Options: A) studied B) study C) have studied
Correct Answer: A) studied
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Past Simple)
Feedback: "Correct! Because Tchamba graduated in 2023, his studies are a finished past action. We must use the Past Simple tense, 'studied'. We can't use 'have studied' in this context (which implies it's ongoing or recent without a specific date)."

QUESTION 2
Dialogue: Mrs. Ekane: "And where ___ you currently live?" Tchamba: "I live in the Bonabéri quartier."
Options: A) are B) does C) do
Correct Answer: C) do
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)
Feedback: "That's right! To ask a 'Wh-' question (like 'Where') with the subject 'you' and the verb 'live', we must use the auxiliary 'do'. The structure is: QW + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb."

QUESTION 3
Dialogue: Mrs. Ekane: "This job requires good computer skills. ___ you use Microsoft Excel well?" Tchamba: "Yes, I am very comfortable with Excel."
Options: A) Can B) Do C) Are
Correct Answer: A) Can
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Modals)
Feedback: "Good choice. We use the modal verb 'Can' to ask about ability or skill. 'Do you use...' is also grammatically correct, but 'Can you use...' is a more common way to ask if someone has the skill."

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: Tchamba: "In my last job, I ___ responsible for managing all the office files." Mrs. Ekane: "That is good experience."
Options: A) am B) were C) was
Correct Answer: C) was
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Past Simple)
Feedback: "Correct! Tchamba is talking about his 'last job' (a finished past situation). The past tense of 'I am' is 'I was'."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: Mrs. Ekane: "This is a busy office. The person for this job ___ be very organized." Tchamba: "I am a very organized person."
Options: A) must to B) must C) should to
Correct Answer: B) must
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Modals)
Feedback: "Perfect! We use 'must' to show a strong requirement. After modal verbs (must, can, should), we always use the base verb ('be') without 'to'. The error 'must to be' comes from translating the French infinitive."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: Tchamba: "I am a fast learner and I am not afraid ___ hard work." Mrs. Ekane: "Good. We need someone who is motivated."
Options: A) of B) from C) for
Correct Answer: A) of
Grammar Category: Prepositions
Feedback: "That's right. The correct English expression is 'to be afraid of' something. This is a common error for French speakers, who might say 'afraid from' (from 'peur de...')."

QUESTION 7
Dialogue: Mrs. Ekane: "Do you have any questions for me?" Tchamba: "Yes. ___ the company offer health insurance?"
Options: A) Do B) Is C) Does
Correct Answer: C) Does
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)
Feedback: "Excellent! The subject of the question is 'the company' (it). To ask a question with 'he', 'she', or 'it', we must use the auxiliary verb 'Does'."

QUESTION 8
Dialogue: Mrs. Ekane: "That's a good question. Yes, we provide ___ for all employees." Tchamba: "That is wonderful to hear."
Options: A) an health insurance B) health insurance C) the health insurance
Correct Answer: B) health insurance
Grammar Category: Articles
Feedback: "Correct! 'Health insurance' (like 'information' or 'advice') is an uncountable noun. We cannot use 'a' or 'an' with it. We must use the zero article (Ø) or 'some'."

Feedback: "Excellent! The subject 'the printing' is a singular activity (it). In the present tense, 'he', 'she', or 'it' must take an '-s' on the verb. So, 'it costs'."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: Attendant: "No, the price is 150. It's 300 total." You: "Okay, fine. ___ I pay with Orange Money?"
Options: A) Do B) Can C) Am
Correct Answer: B) Can
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Modals / Questions)
Feedback: "Good! We use 'Can' to ask for permission ('Can I pay...?') or to ask about ability. This is the most common way to ask about payment methods."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: Attendant: "Sorry, our machine for MoMo ___." You: "What? Why?" Attendant: "It has not worked since this morning."
Options: A) not work B) doesn't work C) not works
Correct Answer: B) doesn't work
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Negatives)
Feedback: "That's right! To make a negative sentence in the present simple, we must use an auxiliary verb. The subject 'our machine' (it) needs 'doesn't' (does not). Saying 'it not work' is a common error from French ('il ne marche pas')."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SCENARIO #9: CHURCH/COMMUNITY MEETING ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Context: You are attending the weekly Sunday service, which is followed by a short community meeting at the church hall to discuss an upcoming tontine (community savings meeting).
Characters:
	•	Pastor Tchinda (Leading the meeting)
	•	Ngono (A community member)
	•	You (Attending the meeting)
Icon: ⛪

QUESTION 1
Dialogue: Pastor Tchinda: "Thank you all for staying. Our meeting will begin ___ a few minutes." You: (to your neighbour) "I hope it is not too long."
Options: A) in B) at C) on
Correct Answer: A) in
Grammar Category: Prepositions
Feedback: "Correct! We use 'in' to talk about a period of time in the future. 'In a few minutes' means the meeting will start after a few minutes have passed."

QUESTION 2
Dialogue: Pastor Tchinda: "Before we discuss the tontine, Mama Biya ___ an announcement about the church bazaar." Mama Biya: "Thank you, Pastor..."
Options: A) have B) has C) are having
Correct Answer: B) has
Grammar Category: Subject-Verb Agreement
Feedback: "That's right! The subject 'Mama Biya' (she) is third-person singular, so she 'has' an announcement. We use 'have' for I, you, we, and they."

QUESTION 3
Dialogue: You: (whispering to Ngono) "I ___ Pastor Tchinda at the market yesterday." Ngono: "Oh, really? He is a very busy man."
Options: A) saw B) have seen C) see
Correct Answer: A) saw
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Past Simple)
Feedback: "Perfect! The word 'yesterday' tells us this is a finished past action. We must use the Past Simple tense, 'saw'. We cannot use 'have seen' with a specific past time like 'yesterday'."

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: Pastor Tchinda: "This tontine is important. We must all show ___ to our neighbours in need." You: "That is true."
Options: A) a kindness B) the kindness C) kindness
Correct Answer: C) kindness
Grammar Category: Articles (Zero Article)
Feedback: "Correct! 'Kindness' is an abstract noun, like 'love', 'life', or 'honesty'. When we talk about these concepts in general, we use the zero article (Ø) (no article). This is a common error from French, which would say 'la gentillesse' ('the kindness')."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: Ngono: (to you) "I ___ about last week's meeting. I was in Limbe." You: "Ah, that's why. They discussed the new date."
Options: A) not knew B) didn't know C) don't know
Correct Answer: B) didn't know
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Negatives)
Feedback: "That's right! The time is 'last week' (past). To make a negative sentence in the past, we must use the auxiliary 'didn't' + base verb ('know'). 'Don't know' is for the present."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: Pastor Tchinda: "Thank you, Mr. Foncha, for your donation. That was a ___." Mr. Foncha: "It is my pleasure to help."
Options: A) gesture very kind B) very kind gesture C) kind gesture very
Correct Answer: B) very kind gesture
Grammar Category: Word Order (Adjectives)
Feedback: "Excellent! In English, the adjective ('kind') and its modifier ('very') both come before the noun ('gesture'). The order 'gesture very kind' is a direct translation from French."

QUESTION 7
Dialogue: You: (to Ngono) "I need some ___ on how the tontine works." Ngono: "Don't worry, I can explain it to you after the meeting."
Options: A) informations B) information C) an information
Correct Answer: B) information
Grammar Category: Plurality (Uncountable)
Feedback: "Perfect! 'Information' is an uncountable noun in English. It never takes an '-s' and you cannot use 'an' with it. This is a very common error from French, which uses 'des informations' (plural)."

Context: It's a Saturday afternoon in the quartier. You decide to visit your neighbor, Mama Atangana, to say hello and return a bowl she lent you. You knock on her door.
Characters:
	•	You (The visitor)
	•	Mama Atangana (The neighbor)
Icon: 🏠

QUESTION 1
Dialogue: You: "Good afternoon, Mama! ___ you busy right now?" Mama Atangana: "Ah, mon ami! No, no. Come in, come in. I am just watching TV."
Options: A) Do B) Are C) Is
Correct Answer: B) Are
Grammar Category: Auxiliaries (Questions)
Feedback: "Correct! We use 'Are' to ask a question about a state of being ('busy'). The subject 'you' pairs with 'are'. We use 'Do' for actions (e.g., 'Do you want...?')."

QUESTION 2
Dialogue: Mama Atangana: "Please, sit down. My son ___ some fufu and eru." You: "Oh, it smells wonderful!"
Options: A) cook B) cooks C) is cooking
Correct Answer: C) is cooking
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Present Continuous)
Feedback: "That's right! Because the action is happening right now (you can smell it), we use the Present Continuous tense: 'is cooking'."

QUESTION 3
Dialogue: You: "Thank you for letting me borrow this bowl. I ___ it last night for the ndolé." Mama Atangana: "It's no problem at all."
Options: A) used B) use C) have used
Correct Answer: A) used
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Past Simple)
Feedback: "Correct! The time 'last night' is a finished past time. For actions with a finished time, we must use the Past Simple tense, 'used'."

QUESTION 4
Dialogue: Mama Atangana: "Would you like some water?" You: "Yes, please. It is ___ day today."
Options: A) a very hot B) a hot very C) very a hot
Correct Answer: A) a very hot
Grammar Category: Word Order (Adjectives)
Feedback: "Perfect! The correct English order is 'a' + (adverb) + (adjective) + (noun). So, we say 'a very hot day'. This order is often different from French."

QUESTION 5
Dialogue: You: "How is your family? I ___ your husband in a long time." Mama Atangana: "He is fine. He is on a mission in Garoua."
Options: A) don't see B) didn't see C) haven't seen
Correct Answer: C) haven't seen
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Present Perfect)
Feedback: "Excellent choice! When you talk about a period of time from the past until now ('in a long time'), we use the Present Perfect tense: 'haven't seen'. 'Didn't see' is for one specific past moment (e.g., 'I didn't see him yesterday')."

QUESTION 6
Dialogue: Mama Atangana: "You must stay and eat with us!" You: "Thank you, Mama. I ___ say no to eru!"
Options: A) can't to B) not can C) can't
Correct Answer: C) can't
Grammar Category: Tense & Form (Modals)
Feedback: "That's right! The negative of 'can' is 'can't' (cannot). After a modal verb, we use the base verb 'say' without 'to'. The error 'can't to say' comes from translating the French infinitive."
